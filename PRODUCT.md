# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors exploring Hugh Scott's projects, experience, and contact details on phones or desktop computers, including keyboard and screen-reader users.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that feels like Hugh's pocket dimension on the internet. Visitors should be able to explore his work and reach him quickly, including on poor Wi-Fi.

## Brand Personality

Personal, mechanical, playful. The existing README establishes the reference: a personal NixOS/GNOME workstation with macOS window controls and an oh-my-posh inspired shell.

## Anti-references

Generic portfolio templates, decorative feature-card grids, and effects that obscure readable content or slow the site. Preserve the established workstation identity during audit fixes.

## Design Principles

- Keep the terminal metaphor approachable through clear instructions and direct contact links.
- Preserve the sky photograph, ASCII signature, and distinctive shell prompt.
- Make the experience responsive and fast on poor connections.
- Keep meaningful content semantic and indexable by search engines.
- Verify improvements in the browser before shipping.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Address the supplied audit's keyboard, assistive-technology, and touch-target findings. Support both themes and reduced motion. Decorative terminal artwork should not obscure semantic identity or navigation.

Context sources: README.md, index.html, the supplied 2026-10-07 design audit, and the user's previously stated portfolio goals.
