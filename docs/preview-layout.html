Rule: one recognisable shape, dead centre, sized so it still reads at 62px. Everything else is atmosphere.
Anatomy — what each slot controls, and what each platform does with it
SlotWhere it comes fromWhat the renderer doesControl you have
Imageog:imageWhatsApp/Telegram/X: 1.91:1 band, cropped to a square (~62px) if the app shows the small variant. LinkedIn: 104x60 thumb.You own the pixels. Keep the focal point centred — edges and a centre-square get cropped.
Titleog:titleBold, 1-2 lines, truncated around 60-74 chars depending on app. Shown on every platform.Full control. This is the only line that survives everywhere, including LinkedIn.
Descriptionog:descriptionTwo lines max, truncated. Hidden on LinkedIn entirely.Full control. 110-160 chars so it fills exactly two lines.
Domainog:urlRendered by the app, not you. WhatsApp tints it green, X greys it.None. It is your domain, so it reads as the brand.
Card backgroundApp themeFollows the reader's light or dark mode. Not controllable.None — so the image carries its own background.
